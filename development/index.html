Our Danish Core

Throughout our history, Danish design and simplicity have been the essence of our furniture, which is handrafted by skilled experts using the highest quality wood.

Embracing modernity has allowed us to evolve and refine our craftmanship to create furniture which combines classical features with contemporary styles and always embodies the Nordic spirit.

In Collaboration

We develop close relationship with architects, designers, cabnet makers and high-end brands, combining our talents with theirs to create exclusive furniture tailored to a particular place or setting.

Noma

In collaboration with Noma's head chef, we designed a furniture line for their new dining room, ensuring the interior experience complements the culinary one and both work together to create an unforgattable flavour of the Nordics.

Arv Collection

The award-winning ARV chair embodies timeless virtues of Danish Modern design while capturing the spirit of a new generation. Featuring high armrests and a hand-woven cord seat, it combines elegance and comfort with a sculpted backrest and fluied design.
